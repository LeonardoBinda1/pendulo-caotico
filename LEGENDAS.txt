FIGURE 3 (main version)
Stroboscopic sections of the driven damped pendulum at Omega=2/3 and driving phase zero. The parameter pairs (q,f) are (a) (0.5,0.9), (b) (0.5,1.07), (c) (0.5,1.2), (d) (0.2,0.8), (e) (0.1,1.2), and (f) (0.05,0.6). Each panel contains 65536 retained samples after 8000 discarded forcing periods, starting from (theta,omega)=(0.2,0), with 480 Runge--Kutta steps per period. Periodic points are shown in blue and chaotic sets in red; the measured largest transverse Lyapunov exponent, per unit dimensionless time, is printed in each panel. Both damping and torque amplitude vary across this comparison; it is not a single bifurcation sequence. No transient points or different sampling phases are superposed.

FIGURE 5
Invariant-manifold crossings and finite-resolution attractor geometry at Omega=2/3 and driving phase zero. (a) Finite portions of the unstable and stable manifolds of the periodic saddle for q=0.05, f=0.12; the rectangle selects the region enlarged in (b). Markers identify two crossings refined by direct shooting and variational equations, with coordinate-plane angles of 10.90 and 8.30 degrees. Their locations are stable under longer local legs and tighter integration tolerance. (c) Stroboscopic attractor for the distinct parameter pair q=0.5, f=1.2, with the selected region magnified in (d); points are thinned only for display. (e) Box counts from 2097152 samples in the fixed coordinates u=(theta_bar+pi)/(2*pi), v=omega/4, using 16 grid origins and periodic angular indices. The shaded fit window is delta=2^(-j), j=6,...,10. (f) Median fitted capacity and information dimensions as the sample grows; bands show the full grid-origin range, not statistical confidence intervals. The dashed line is the Kaplan--Yorke section estimate. The crossings and the fractal dimension belong to different, explicitly labelled parameter regimes. All neighboring fit windows and a matched-sample integration-step comparison are supplied in the additional convergence figure.
